India publishes reliable totals: GDP and its three approaches, value added, consumption, government consumption, investment, exports and imports, the production structure, tax devolution, each sector's saving, the balance of payments, and household financial saving. Inside those totals, many splits are not published. The map fills them, and labels them **estimated**. These are the main ones; each link opens an example on the map.

- **Every split by household group**: income, consumption, taxes, transfers and saving across the ten rural and urban groups. The totals are national; the shares come from household surveys and other studies. Examples: [income earned by households](/en/flow/FACT--HH/), [household consumption](/en/flow/HH--PRDS/), [household saving](/en/flow/HH--SI/).
- **Interest, dividends and other property income between institutions**: who pays whom. Where the split follows the previous national-accounts series it is flagged "estimated with the old-base structure". Examples: [dividends and distributed income from companies](/en/flow/NFC--HH/), [interest and insurance income from financial firms](/en/flow/FC--HH/), [companies' payments to financial firms](/en/flow/NFC--FC/).
- **The split of financial firms' saving and income** among the RBI, banks, NBFCs, and insurers and pension funds. Examples: [financial firms' saving](/en/flow/FC--SI/), [their operating surplus](/en/flow/FACT--FC/).
- **The split between the Centre and the States** of government consumption, saving and shared taxes. Examples: [the Centre's spending on public services](/en/flow/CEN--PRDS/), [the States' spending](/en/flow/STA--PRDS/), [the Centre's negative saving](/en/flow/CEN--SI~neg/).
- **Who holds which financial asset from whom**, taken from RBI's flow of funds, and the residuals that reconcile it with the national accounts. Examples: [financial assets acquired](/en/flow/SI--FINM/), [new liabilities](/en/flow/FINM--SI/).

Some figures are **set by balancing** rather than estimated from a source, and the map labels them so: estimates that balancing moved by enough to need a written explanation, including those whose move is unexplained or explained only in direction, and flows that sit on the edge of a judgement band. For these the flow panel shows the value before balancing, the value after, and the change.

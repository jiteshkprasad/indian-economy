Every number on this site traces back to a public source: a publisher, a table or series, the date we retrieved it, its unit and whether it is in current prices. Most come from MoSPI's national accounts and Supply-Use Tables, the household surveys, the Union and State budgets and finance accounts, the RBI's statistics on the balance of payments, flow of funds and State finances, and the regulators of banks, insurance and pensions. The flow panel on the map lists the sources behind each flow, closest inputs first.

**Links to RBI pages are withheld.** The RBI's website terms do not allow deep links to its pages or files, or caching them, without permission, and that permission has not yet been obtained. Until the RBI grants it, RBI sources are cited by name, publication, table and retrieval date, with no link. The links of <DataFig path="methodology.withheld_urls"/> source records on RBI sites are withheld this way.

**Licences.** No source we use publishes its data under India's open-data licence (GODL) on its own site, so reuse depends on each publisher's terms. MoSPI allows reproduction with prominent acknowledgement. The RBI needs to give permission for two things, not one: to link to and cache its pages and files, and to republish figures derived from its statistics. Its Bulletin allows reproduction with acknowledgement (the older issues we use are assumed to say the same), but its Handbook, balance-of-payments, State finances and other releases state no reuse terms and reserve all rights, and the project downloaded every RBI file for internal processing only (D1-1) until the RBI agrees. This site shows figures the model derives from RBI statistics, among them the balance-of-payments flows, the flow of funds, household financial saving and the remittance survey shares used in the Dubai story. The Ministry of Finance, SEBI, GSTN and DGCI&S also require permission. This is a personal project and no such permissions have been sought: the site shows only figures the model computes from these statistics, credited to each publisher, and does not republish their files or tables (ISSUES P1-9). A few publishers (the Controller General of Accounts, the Sixteenth Finance Commission and PFRDA) state no licence at all; we cite them as they ask, and their figures appear only as part of the model's own computed accounts. Figures adapted from IFPRI's social accounting matrix are used under its open licence; IFPRI has not reviewed or endorsed this adaptation.

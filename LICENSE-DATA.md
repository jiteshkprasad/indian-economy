## Official statistics

The figures are computed from official statistics. Those statistics belong to their publishers (the Ministry of
Statistics and Programme Implementation, the Reserve Bank of India, the Ministry of Finance and others listed in
`data/registry.yaml` and `SOURCES.md`). This licence covers the model's own computed and balanced figures and the
text, not the publishers' original tables. The original tables are not included in this repository; when you
download them, their publishers' terms apply.

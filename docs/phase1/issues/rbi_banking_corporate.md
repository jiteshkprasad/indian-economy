## Framework requests
- `FetchContext` has no public way to ask whether `(source_id, name)` is already pinned, i.e. whether `ctx.fetch` will touch the network. The D1-4 gate here (and in rbi_financial_accounts) reads `ctx.store.pins` to decide whether to wait 10 s. A small `ctx.is_pinned(source_id, name)` helper, or a framework-level per-host minimum gap (e.g. `HOST_MIN_GAP = {"rbidocs.rbi.org.in": 10}` in `fetch.py`), would let units stop touching internals.

**Framework close-out (2026-09-29):** `ctx.is_pinned(source_id, name, url=None)` and a framework per-host gap (`fetch.HOST_MIN_INTERVAL_S`, rbidocs 10 s) plus stop-on-first-challenge: DONE. The unit's own gate still works and can now be simplified. Dispositions: `docs/phase1/FRAMEWORK_REQUESTS.md`.

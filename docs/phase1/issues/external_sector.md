## INR/USD exchange rate: guide for Phase 2

Written for trade_satellites and any other unit that holds US$ figures. DECISIONS D1-8 puts the US$-to-rupee conversion in Phase 2, using this series. The claims below are tested in `tests/units/test_external_sector.py` (`test_fx_*`, including `test_fx_phase2_usage_contract`).

**The dataset.** `external_sector_fx_inr_usd` (`data/clean/external_sector/external_sector_fx_inr_usd.parquet`) has 188 rows: unit `INR_per_USD` (rupees per US dollar, as printed to 4 decimals), `status` official, `estimate_stage` `n.a.`, `price_basis` `n/a`, `stock_flow` `n/a`, `dim_other` `currency=usd`, and `source_id` `rbi_hbs_external`. There is one vintage, 2026-07-31 (HBS 2025-26), and one provenance record per source table (T133 and T205, both HTML pages on www.rbi.org.in).

| series_id | period_type | Coverage | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `t133.usd_fy_average` | `fy` | 1986-87 .. 2025-26 (40) | flows in fiscal years (April-March) |
| `t133.usd_end_march` | `date` | 1987-03-31 .. 2026-03-31 (40) | stocks at end-March |
| `t205.usd_month_average` | `month` | 2022-01 .. 2026-06 (54, no gaps) | flows in months |
| `t205.usd_end_month` | `date` | 2022-01-31 .. 2026-06-30 (54) | stocks at a month-end |

Reference-year anchors: the FY 2023-24 average is 82.7897 and the end-March 2024 rate is 83.3739.

**How to convert (Phase 2).**
1. Declare `DEPENDS = ["external_sector"]` and read the rates with `ctx.clean("external_sector", "external_sector_fx_inr_usd")`. Never read `data/clean` by path.
2. **Filter to exactly one `series_id`** before converting. End-March dates appear in both `t133.usd_end_march` and `t205.usd_end_month` (the values are equal for 2022-2026, which is tested). If you pass the whole dataset, `usd_million_to_inr_crore` stops with "duplicate exchange rates".
3. `units.usd_million_to_inr_crore(values, rates)` joins on `period`, divides by 10 (US$ 1 million x Rs/US$ = Rs crore x 10), sets `unit` to `INR_crore`, and returns `fx_prov_id`. It raises on any period that has no rate. It never falls back.
4. The converted rows are `derived`. Their provenance record needs a `method` (for example, "US$ million x RBI FY-average Rs/US$ (HBS T133) / 10") and `inputs` = the US$ rows' prov_ids plus `fx_prov_id`. The runner checks that an `INR_per_USD` series is among the inputs.
5. Match the rate to the row: flows get the period average, and stocks get the end-period rate. Example: US$ 1,000 million in FY 2023-24 = Rs 8,278.97 crore.

Options for the gaps (none has been done; they are decisions for Phase 2 or the orchestrator):
- (a) Calendar-year averages: HBS T132, which -16 lists as calendar-year rates, is on the same RBI page family but is **not pinned or ingested**, and was not re-checked in this pass. This unit can add it as an official series with one extra fetch if asked.
- (b) Months before 2022: HBS T205 prints only from January 2022. An earlier source (an older HBS edition, or RBI/FBIL reference-rate archives) would be needed. Not checked.
- (c) Averaging 12 monthly rates into a calendar year is `derived`, and possible only for 2022-2025. Shifting FY averages onto calendar years is an approximation. Label it `estimated`, with its method, and never pass it off as official.
- (d) Keep the rows in US$ (the current state; SPEC §6 prefers Rs crore but does not require it).

**Caveats.**
- Rate regimes (T133 notes): official rates up to 1991-92; FEDAI indicative rates from 1992-93 to April 2012; RBI reference rates from May 2012; FBIL reference rates from 10 July 2018. The FY jumps of 1991-92 and 1992-93 (over 25%) are real breaks and are tested as such.
- End-period rows are dated to the last calendar day of the month, but the rate is that of the last working day.
- For BoP, IIP and external debt, use RBI's own printed rupee tables. Do not re-convert the US$ tables. RBI converts quarter by quarter (BoP) or currency by currency (debt), so a single FY rate differs slightly: gross BoP cells are within 3% (tested), and debt is within 0.2% except at end-March 2020 and 2021 (-10).
- Only US$ rates are ingested. T133 and T205 also print SDR, GBP, EUR and JPY, but there is no `INR_per_EUR`-type unit (FRAMEWORK_REQUESTS #4, DEFERRED), so `EUR_million` rows (io_tables) cannot be converted yet.
- A `fetch --refresh` could add a second vintage of the same series (-2). Filter to one vintage as well before joining on `period`.

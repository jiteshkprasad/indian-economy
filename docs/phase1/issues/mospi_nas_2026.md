# mospi_nas_2026: Phase 1 issues

Scope built on 2026-09-29: all 64 NAS 2026 statements (new base 2022-23; 1.1 and 8.17.1 hand-mapped, the other
62 through `stmts2026.py`), a derived dataset of the two official gaps (household residual, saving-investment
errors and omissions), the NAS 2024 old-base sequences of accounts (1.15, 2.0, 2.1, 2.1A, 2.1B, 2.2, 3.0, 3.1,
3.2, 4.0, 5.0, 6.0; 2011-12 to 2022-23) and NAS 2025 old-base Statements 1.1, 1.9, 1.14, 5.3 (2011-12 to
2023-24). 81 datasets, 26,805 values (17,756 new base incl. 6 derived; 9,049 old base). Every statement is checked against a frozen row spec
(`pipeline/ingest/mospi_nas_2026/spec_nas2026.json`, `spec_oldbase.json`).

**Follow-up, 2026-09-29 (Phase 1 close-out):** the NAS API (`mospi_nas_api`, api.mospi.gov.in through the D1-6
legacy-TLS opt-in) is ingested: five datasets `mospi_nas_2026_api_*`, 15,775 values: the old-base back series
1950-51 to 2011-12 (5,344), the old-base annual series by revision round 2011-12 to 2025-26 (7,160) and quarterly
2011-12 to 2025-26 (2,084), and the new-base annual (575) and quarterly series to Q1 2026-27 (612). The F1 identity
is written as a derived row (+3). 4.1 States now take their code from `pipeline/core/geo.state_code`. The SNA
code-column guard rejects one-sided codes (verifier s2-1). Old-base estimate stages are corrected from the API's
revision labels (issue 9). **Now 86 datasets, 42,583 values.** Code: `pipeline/ingest/mospi_nas_2026/api.py`;
tests: `tests/units/test_mospi_nas_2026_api.py`. Issues 29-37 below (37: verifier c-1, API GCF item codes).

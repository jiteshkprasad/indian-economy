## 1. How to read the tables

- **A and B** are the two sources' values in Rs crore, current prices. "A-B" is the raw difference, and "% of B" is that difference as a share of B.
- **Bridge** lists named reconciling items, each taken from a clean dataset (for example, the CIF adjustment on imports). **Residual** = A - (B + bridge).
- **Tolerance** is a percentage of |B|, with a floor of Rs 5 crore for rounding in published tables.
- **Verdicts:**
  - **agree**: no bridge needed, and |A-B| is within tolerance.
  - **explained (bridged)**: sourced bridge items close the gap to within tolerance.
  - **explained**, with no bridge: a documented coverage, vintage or concept difference keeps the gap inside a stated tolerance. The bridging items themselves are not in the clean data, so the size of the explanation is plausible but not proven.
  - **explained (ratio)**: the HCES-to-PFCE ratio lies in the expected band.
  - **UNEXPLAINED**: no verified cause. The test asserts the tolerance we would expect and is marked `xfail` (non-strict). It shows up in every test run, and it will XPASS once a fix lands.
- Each detail line after a table gives the exact dataset, series id and period for every term, plus the reasoning.

## 2. Summary

Across 205 checks, 145 agree, 37 are explained and 23 are UNEXPLAINED (close-out refresh; it was 194 / 136 / 37 / 21 before 11 checks were added).

### What holds together

- **The new-base production core is internally exact.** GDP, GVA, product taxes and subsidies, the final-use columns, total output and total intermediate consumption in NAS 2026 Statement 1.1/7.1 equal the Supply-Use Table to within Rs 3 crore, in both 2022-23 and 2023-24. Output, IC, GVA and CE agree for all 11 NAS industry groups, with one exception: SUT industry 58 ("Education & Research") contains R&D, which NAS files under professional services. NAS 8.13's "professional, scientific and technical services incl. R&D" output (Rs 1,56,915 cr in 2023-24; 1,11,186 cr in 2022-23) closes that gap exactly (IND-re-output, IND-os-output). So Phase 2 can take the SUT as the benchmark without first reconciling it to NAS.
- **NAS 6.1 and the RBI BoP (BPM6, Rs crore) agree exactly once three named bridges are applied:**
  - the CIF adjustment on imports of services (Rs 2,12,499 cr in 2023-24; 2,92,776 cr in 2022-23). This is the X_DISC cell D2.
  - NAS books general-government transfers with the rest of the world in the *capital* account. EXT-14 matches the NAS item to the BoP "official transfers" line exactly (-7,000).
  - goods sent for processing (Rs 11,963 cr).

  Net lending to the rest of the world, primary income, remittances and FDI are identical. The FSF rest-of-world row equals the BoP financial account within 0.05% in 2023-24 and 0.07% in 2022-23.
- **Tax devolution closes.** The Union's "share of net proceeds assigned to States" (Finance Accounts minor head 901: Rs 11,29,494 cr) equals the States' receipt in the RBI State Finances (11,29,761) and in FC16 (11,29,397) within 0.02%. It also closes for each tax separately.
- **The fiscal documents agree with each other:**
  - Union taxes: Receipt Budget gross = Finance Accounts net + States' share, for CGST, corporation tax, income tax and customs.
  - Union interest payments: Finance Accounts = Annual Financial Statement (Rs 11,01,868 cr).
  - State interest payments: FC16 (28 States) = RBI State Finances (all States and UTs) once the interest of Delhi, J&K and Puducherry is removed (residual 0.3%).
  - State grants: the same bridge works using the three UTs' published shares (residual Rs 32 cr).
  - SGST: FC16 KFI = the FC16 State Finance Accounts major head 0006, to the rupee.
- **The RBI surplus lines up.** The Union's 2023-24 receipt (Finance Accounts 0050-106: Rs 87,416.22 cr) equals the RBI Annual Report's surplus for RBI year 2022-23 exactly (DECISIONS D0-7). The FSF records the accrued surplus as an RBI payable to the Centre. Its 2023-24 flow (1,21,648) is within 1.5% of the change in "surplus payable" (1,23,458; K9b).
- **Household financial saving:** NAS 5.3 equals RBI Bulletin Table 52(a) within 0.03% in both years.
- **Late additions close exactly.** The NAS 2026 figures from the eSankhyiki API equal Statement 1.1 for GDP, PFCE and GFCE in both years (GDP-7/8/9). Grants for creation of capital assets tie across two publishers: Expenditure Budget Statement 6 Actuals 2023-24 and the Finance Accounts IGAS-2 capital column both give Rs 3,03,916 cr, a difference of Rs 0.01 cr (GOV-14). Union GPF gross additions (68,989) and discharges (63,930) in Finance Accounts Statement 14 equal the AFS public account (GOV-17/18).
- **HCES vs NAS PFCE:** the HCES aggregate (12 x persons x average MPCE, rural + urban) is Rs 72.7 lakh cr in 2023-24. That is **44.3%** of NAS PFCE (Rs 164.2 lakh cr); the 2022-23 ratio is 44.2%. With imputed free and PDS items it is 45.3%. This matches the expected ~44% (GAP_MAP H1).

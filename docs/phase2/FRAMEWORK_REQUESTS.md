## Deviations from DESIGN_BRIEF.md, and why (framework agent, 2026-09-30)

The brief says the guide wins once it exists; each point below is also stated in `docs/phase2/SAM_GUIDE.md`.

| # | Brief says | Framework does | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Production entries all `fixed`/official (s.4 table) | Entries built from SUT cells (P1–P6, I3) are **`anchored`**, each allowed to move by ₹1 crore per SUT cell aggregated into it (`tol_rel = n_cells / |value|`). The tax vectors P8, P9, P10 and the D4 zeros stay `fixed`. Status stays `official`. Production's constraint targets get the same allowance (`tol_abs = n_cells` crore) | The published SUT is not exactly additive (many cells carry decimals, so this is not a whole-crore rounding bound; corrected in the Wave A review, V-3): supply − use per product misses by up to ₹0.5 crore, industry output − inputs by up to ₹0.86 crore, and the TTM column sums to +₹0.33 crore. ## Wave B solver review (2026-09-30): findings and dispositions

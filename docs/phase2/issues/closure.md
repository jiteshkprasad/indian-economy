## Assumptions (each also a provenance record)

- K4a = pure residual of each group's current account (option A); sum constrained to NAS 1.9 by the K4 constraint during balancing (record per group, `estimated`).
- D1 total = D2 + D3 + D4 as written (DECISIONS D2-3; `derived` record over the other blocks' X_DISC records and the published residual); D1 split by prior gross income, remainder on the largest group (`estimated`, fixed; the record cites the income-side entries of the key and the D1 total record, V-CLO-4).
- The K4 constraint duplicates capital_financial's (same rows, cols, refs [K4a], target and kind): the framework merges it as a duplicate (tested with a synthetic capital_financial constraint).
- Plausibility band for group saving: [-25%, +50%] of gross income (plan s.2.1 step 8), findings only.
- Multi-source records (K4a, D1 split, diagnostics) carry `source_id = mospi_nas_2026_publication`: the NAS totals every closure number is tied to (the framework requires one named source when inputs span several). A diagnostic record whose inputs do not include a NAS record takes the source most of its inputs come from.
- Satellite statuses (V-CLO-3, `STATUS_RULE` in the code): a row is `estimated` where any entry, constraint target or clean input it adds up is estimated (even where a total happens to match a published figure); `derived` where all of them are official or derived, and for counts of entries; `official` only for a row that copies one published figure or an official constraint target unchanged (hh_saving `all.k4_nas`, institution_gaps `nas_total`, constraint_gaps `target` of an official constraint). hh_saving copies keep the status of what they copy (persons: hh_keys `derived`; IFPRI rates: clean `estimated`; `all.d1`: the `derived` D1 total). One record per status per satellite. `xdisc_identity` stays `derived`: capital_financial's split of D3 over the K_* accounts is estimated, but the table uses only the D3 total, which keeps the published S-I errors (check `d3.written_vs_nas`).

## Assumptions (each also a provenance record)

- **Survey window:** HCES 2023-24 shares apply to FY 2023-24 levels (record "Survey-window assumption").
- **H1:** uniform scaling within each SUT product (every C1a/C4d record).
- **NPISH embedded** in each product's PFCE and distributed with its key (C1a/C4a/C4b/C4d records; C4c memo record).
- **C4a split:** owner + tenant rent = SUT p144 PFCE − SUT supply by industry 67; actual/imputed ratio = HCES V520/(V520+V539) = 0.250231; keys HCES 520 (actual, govt proxy) and 539 (imputed).
- **FISIM reference rate** r* = SCB return on investments (record `fisim_split`, copied to `hh_keys` fisim_params).
- **AIDIS mapping:** AIDIS household-expenditure quintile classes and HCES person quintiles rank households alike; overlap by cumulative household share (record `aidis`); the deposit ratio of `aidis_deposits` is capped at 1 (that key is published, not used by C4b); equity/MF/small-savings keys = financial-assets proxy (record `aidis_proxy`).
- **S8 unit value:** uniform national GFCE unit value per grain (quantity shares × SUT GFCE).
- **MPCE placement model** for `hh_state_xwalk` and `hh_national_quintile_xwalk` (log-uniform within classes, log-midpoint boundaries).
- **HCES/SUT ratio allocation:** an item that keys several products is split in proportion to their SUT PFCE (`c1a_hces_nas_ratio` only).

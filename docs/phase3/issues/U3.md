## Resume run (2026-10-02, ~12:10–13:20): decisions and open items

Decisions to review (V2/V3/V5):
- **Split-node children are not balanced on their own.** At level 0 the two copies of PRDS (and PRODUCERS) have equal heights (tested on the real export: 677.82 and 369.09 lakh crore). Once PRDS is expanded, each commodity's payer copy and receiver copy differ, because trade and transport margins circulate inside product markets and stay internal at every zoom (PLAN §4.5.7). The layout shows this honestly; the invariant test checks copy equality only for the level-0 split node.
- **Top-flows board slots:** `slot_share` is used as a floor. With ports reserved per ribbon at its own largest year (so nothing moves while scrubbing), the per-ribbon maxima can add up to more than any one year's total (real data: Saving and investment in 2011-12), so a slot is max(slot_share, Σ reserved). Ribbons keep their own port positions in every year (tested for all 76 years).
- **Node panel partners:** "money in / money out" of a node lists partners by group: the node's siblings, its ancestors' siblings, then the other level-0 groups (never an unnamed "rest of the economy").
- **Permalink pages embed only their own data subset** (the link's cells, entries, provenance records, matched series). Subsets are passed straight to the panel and never prime the shared client cache, so client navigation to the Map or a story cannot read a subset by mistake.
- **First screen on phones:** household-outlay labels were shortened ("Goods and services", "Interest and finance", "Other to government", "Not allocated") and the badges in that list use a smaller font, so the h1, both headline cards and the first story card fit in 375×812 (measured: story card bottom at 783 px with U2's 77 px header). The series-break notes under the slider are technical (exported source ids); they sit in a collapsed "Breaks in the series this year" disclosure.
- In controlled mode (stories) the diagram marks carry no ARIA attributes (axe `aria-prohibited-attr`); the figure is one labelled group and the stories' highlighted-flow list is the text alternative.

Open / not done:

- Trend charts draw one line across a base change, with the break marked by a dashed line and a note; the line is not split at the break.
- Full keyboard model is implemented and tested in Chromium and WebKit (map.spec on webkit-desktop passed); a manual screen-reader walk-through is left to V3.

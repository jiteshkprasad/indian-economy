# U5 issues (Stories)

## Deviations from PLAN (with reasons)

1. **GST devolution is apportioned by tax in the SAM (PLAN §4.9 says it is not).** The entries `G_STA <- G_CEN`, ref `R1`, carry components `income_tax`, `cgst`, `corporation_tax`, `customs`, `union_excise`, `other_commodity_taxes`, `service_tax` (prior_status official; Finance Accounts tax-wise devolution, FC16 KFI total). The GST story therefore shows the official CGST devolution (`devol_cgst`, about ₹3.43 lakh crore) and the share of CGST excluding UTGST it represents, with the caveat that a year's devolution carries adjustments for earlier years. V2 please check the wording in `web/content/en/stories/gst-rupee.mdx` step `onwards`.
2. **Phone map in stories keeps the step's own preset** (with `onlyHighlighted` and a fixed 280 px height) instead of forcing `preset="simple"` (PLAN §4.9). Steps that zoom (e.g. `PRDS--TAX.GST`, `FINM.GOVD--SI.GOV`) name links that do not exist in the simple preset, so forcing it would draw nothing. The inline list of highlighted flows is shown on every viewport.
3. **`components/story/index.ts` does not export `StoryFig`** (PLAN §4.1 lists it). The real `StoryFig` is a server component that reads `<v>/stories/<slug>.json` at build time through `@/data/server` (`server-only`); `index.ts` is imported by client code (U3's `FirstScreen` uses `StoryCards`), so exporting it there would break client bundles. Import it by path: `@/components/story/StoryFig`. The story page passes it per page (PLAN §3.9).
4. **"What households do with their income" uses the average of all households**, not a remittance-weighted mix of the receiving groups. The text says so ("Indian households, taken together") and states that these are average, not marginal, propensities.
5. **Interest in the deficit story uses the published series F25 and F26** (government accounts, cash), not SAM cells; F25 has two periods and its SAM check verdict is `explained`, so it is shown as two values, never as a trend (PLAN §3.7.4). The text points to the Map's series panel for the definitional difference.
6. **"Who pays at the till" shows five use types** (households, investment, firms' non-creditable inputs, government purchases, valuables). p7b has no `exports` or `cii` rows for the GST accounts (exports are zero-rated), so the five add up to the GST total (checked in `tests/export/test_export_stories.py`).
7. **`prorata_sum`** (imports and trade margins in household consumption, remittance story step `buys`) is a figure kind I asked U1 for; U1 implemented it (READY: stories-eval). It is labelled estimated ("proportionality assumption").
8. **GST levels are read on the receipt side** (resumed run). `gst_total`, `cgst_total`, `sgst_total`, `cess_total` are now `G_CEN/G_STA <- T_*` (what each government collects), not `T_* <- C_*`. The values are identical (each tax account pays one government and balances; tested), but the receipt entries are the members of the judgement bands `taxes:t1.cgst` / `taxes:t1.gces`, so CGST and the cess now carry `judgement_band` and "set by balancing", as the story text says (balanced CGST and cess sit exactly at the upper band edge). Before, they were labelled plain "estimated" with no flag, which contradicted the text.
9. **Product and till shares use a commodity-side denominator** (`gst_commodity_side`, not shown on its own; `check_equals` against `gst_total`). The evaluator gives a ratio the least certain label of its parts, so with the receipt-side total every share would read "set by balancing" because of the CGST level, although the shares come from the estimated supply-and-use split. With the commodity-side denominator the till shares read "estimated" and the product shares "estimated", except construction, which balancing moved ("set by balancing", level unexplained); the text now says so and the test pins it.
